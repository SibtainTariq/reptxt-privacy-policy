This Privacy Policy describes how Rep Txt ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Rep Txt does not require you to create an account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or the text content you enter into the App. All text processing is performed locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to collect data about your device and your interaction with ads in order to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, and to measure ad performance.

Data that may be collected by AdMob and its partners includes:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • Mobile advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners, not by us directly. We do not have access to or control over this data beyond aggregated, anonymized ad performance reporting.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • You can also reset your advertising identifier from the same menu

Even if you opt out of personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized advertisements in the App.

Data Security

We do not store your data on external servers. Since all text processing happens locally on your device, there is no risk of your text content being accessed, transmitted, or leaked by us.

Children's Privacy

Rep Txt is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.
